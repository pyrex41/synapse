## Service Health

| Metric | Target | Actual | Status |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Overall CSAT score | > 4.0 / 5.0 | 4.1 | On target |
| NPS score | > 30 | 34 | On target |
| Survey response rate | > 15% | 18.2% | On target |
| Support resolution CSAT | > 4.0 / 5.0 | 3.9 | Below target |
| Feature adoption satisfaction | > 4.0 / 5.0 | 4.3 | On target |

Survey was distributed to 4,800 active portal users in Q1 2025 and received 874 responses (18.2% response rate). Overall CSAT of 4.1 / 5.0 meets the target. Support resolution satisfaction at 3.9 is the one area below threshold.

## Key Highlights

- **Account management rated highest**: Self-service account management features scored 4.5 / 5.0. Customers specifically praised the preference center and notification controls.
- **Search feature requested by 41% of respondents**: "Ability to search my account history and documents" was the top feature request by a significant margin. This aligns with the Customer Portal Search Integration TDD in progress.
- **Support ticket resolution time is the top complaint**: 28% of respondents cited slow support ticket response time as their primary dissatisfaction. Median first-response time is currently 18 hours against a 12-hour target.

## Active Initiatives

1. **Support ticket SLA improvement**: Support team is trialing an auto-assignment rule change to reduce first-response time. Target: 12 hours median by end of Q2.
2. **Search feature scoping**: Survey data is being used to prioritize the search feature in the Q2 roadmap.
3. **Dashboard redesign user testing**: 12 customers from the survey pool have agreed to participate in dashboard redesign usability testing.

## Incidents

No incidents related to survey collection or data handling.

## Risks

- **Medium**: Support ticket CSAT below threshold. If first-response time does not improve by Q2, NPS may decline.
- **Low**: Response rate of 18.2% is above target but concentrated among highly active users; results may not reflect the full customer base.

## Next Month Focus

- Monitor support ticket first-response time improvement
- Complete dashboard redesign user testing
- Include search prioritization findings in Q2 roadmap planning session
