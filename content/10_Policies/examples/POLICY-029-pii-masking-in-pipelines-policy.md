## Scope

This policy governs the handling of personally identifiable information (PII) within all data pipelines operated by the engineering organization. It applies to any pipeline stage that processes fields classified as PII under applicable data protection regulations, including names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, device identifiers, and financial identifiers.

## Rationale

- PII exposed in pipeline logs, intermediate storage, or downstream datasets creates regulatory liability under GDPR, CCPA, and similar frameworks
- Data breaches involving PII in analytics infrastructure are increasingly common and damaging
- Masking at ingestion prevents PII from proliferating into downstream systems that lack appropriate access controls
- Consistent masking standards enable safe use of production-like data in non-production environments

## Policy Statements

- PII fields must be masked or tokenized before data exits the ingestion layer; raw PII must not be written to the data lake or warehouse
- Masking operations must use approved, reversible tokenization for fields requiring re-identification for authorized use cases
- Pipeline code that handles PII fields must undergo a privacy review before deployment to production
- Logs generated by pipelines processing PII must not contain PII values; structured logging must scrub sensitive fields
- Non-production environments must use synthetically generated or fully anonymized PII substitutes
- Violations of this policy must be reported to the data protection officer within 24 hours of discovery
