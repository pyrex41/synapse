## Scope

This policy defines the retention periods and disposal requirements for all data generated by warehouse operations, including stock movement logs, receiving records, cycle count results, shipment manifests, and warehouse event streams. It applies to all warehouse locations, third-party logistics (3PL) partners, and any system that stores warehouse-origin data on behalf of the organization.

## Rationale

- Regulatory requirements in applicable jurisdictions mandate minimum retention periods for inventory and logistics records
- Audit and dispute resolution processes require access to historical movement logs to trace the origin of discrepancies
- Retaining data longer than necessary increases storage costs and expands the blast radius of potential data breaches
- Consistent retention rules prevent ad-hoc data deletion that could compromise compliance posture

## Policy Statements

- Raw stock movement event logs must be retained for a minimum of 7 years to satisfy financial audit requirements
- Warehouse receiving records and shipment manifests must be retained for 5 years
- Cycle count snapshots and inventory reconciliation reports must be retained for 3 years
- Data marked for deletion must be purged within 30 days of its retention expiry using approved secure deletion methods
- All data subject to a legal hold must be preserved indefinitely until the hold is formally lifted, regardless of standard retention periods
- Retention schedules must be documented and reviewed annually by the CISO and legal counsel
