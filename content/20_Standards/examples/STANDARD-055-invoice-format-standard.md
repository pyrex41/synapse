## Area

This standard defines the required structure, content, and rendering format for all invoices generated by the Billing Engine. It applies to PDF invoices delivered to customers, invoice data returned via the billing API, and invoice records stored in the billing database.

Compliance with this standard ensures invoices are legally valid across supported jurisdictions, readable by customers, and machine-parseable for downstream integrations.

## Controls

- Every invoice must include: invoice ID, issue date, due date, customer legal name, customer billing address, line items with unit price and quantity, subtotal, applicable taxes with jurisdiction and rate, total amount due, and currency code
- Invoice IDs must be unique, sequential within an account, and formatted as `INV-{ACCOUNT_ID}-{YYYYMM}-{SEQ}` (e.g., `INV-A1234-202501-0042`)
- All monetary amounts must be represented with exactly two decimal places in the invoice display format
- Tax line items must be itemized per jurisdiction and must not be aggregated into a single "tax" line
- Invoice PDF rendering must produce output that passes accessibility checks (WCAG 2.1 AA) for screen readers

## Compliance Mappings

- SOC 2 CC6.1: Logical access and data integrity controls for financial outputs
- PCI-DSS 3.4: Rendering of cardholder data in invoices must be masked except for last four digits
- GDPR Article 17: Invoice records containing EU customer PII must be marked for retention policy enforcement
