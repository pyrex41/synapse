## Summary

Enable customers to customize the appearance and content of invoices generated by the Billing Engine. Enterprise customers frequently require invoices to include their company logo, custom PO numbers, specific remit-to addresses, and custom footer text for internal accounting workflows. Currently all invoices use a static company-branded template with no customer-configurable fields. This initiative adds a customization layer to the Invoice Generation Pipeline ([[TDD-047|TDD-047]]) and Billing Webhook Processor ([[TDD-050|TDD-050]]). Invoice formatting compliance is governed by [[STANDARD-058|STANDARD-058]].

## Goals

- Unblock enterprise deals that require invoice customization as a procurement requirement
- Reduce manual invoice re-issue requests handled by Customer Success (currently 80/month)
- Comply with [[STANDARD-058|STANDARD-058]] invoice content requirements across all customer tiers
- Provide customers with the flexibility to match invoices to their internal procurement workflows

## In Scope

- Customer logo upload (PNG/SVG, displayed in invoice header)
- Custom PO number field per subscription (appears on all invoices for that subscription)
- Custom remit-to / billing address overrides (separate from account address)
- Custom invoice footer text (up to 500 characters, plain text)
- Preview of customized invoice before saving
- Customization settings page in account billing settings

## Out of Scope

- Full white-label / custom domain invoice delivery
- Per-invoice customization (customization is per-account, applied to all invoices)
- Custom invoice line item labels or terminology
- Invoice templates with drag-and-drop layout editors

## Users and Flows

**Enterprise account admin**: Configures the customization settings once and expects all future invoices to apply them automatically. They access settings via the billing portal, upload their logo, enter their PO number and custom footer, save, and preview a rendered sample invoice before finalizing.

**Finance staff downloading invoices**: They do not interact with the customization settings but receive the benefit — invoices with their company's logo and PO number can be processed immediately by their accounts payable team without manual editing.

## Requirements

- Allow account admin to upload a company logo (PNG or SVG, max 500KB) that appears in the invoice header
- Allow account admin to set a default PO number that appears on all invoices for the account
- Allow account admin to set a custom billing address (for the "bill to" section of the invoice, separate from the Stripe customer address)
- Allow account admin to set custom footer text (max 500 characters, plain text, no HTML)
- Show a live preview of the customized invoice before saving changes
- Apply customization settings to all new invoices generated after the settings are saved
- Allow regeneration of the most recent invoice PDF with updated customization (for customers who update settings after receiving the month's invoice)

## KPIs

- **Manual re-issue reduction**: Invoice re-issue requests decrease from 80/month to < 20/month within 3 months
- **Enterprise deal enablement**: Invoice customization no longer cited as a deal blocker in sales pipeline (measured via sales opportunity notes)
- **Adoption**: 30% of enterprise accounts configure at least logo or PO number within 6 months of launch
- **Preview usage**: 70% of accounts that save customization settings use the preview before saving

## Information Architecture

- Customization settings accessible at `app.example.com/billing/settings/invoice`
- Logo files stored in S3 under `customer-assets/{account_id}/invoice-logo`
- Customization applied by the Invoice PDF service at generation time
- TDD reference for Invoice Pipeline: [[TDD-047|TDD-047]]
- TDD reference for Webhook Processor: [[TDD-050|TDD-050]]
- Compliance requirements: [[STANDARD-058|STANDARD-058]]

## Data Model

- **InvoiceCustomization**: `account_id`, `logo_s3_key`, `logo_content_type`, `po_number`, `billing_address_override`, `footer_text`, `updated_at`, `updated_by`
- **InvoiceCustomizationHistory**: append-only log of all changes to `InvoiceCustomization` (who changed what and when)

## Non-Functional

- Logo image must be validated server-side for file type and rendered in the PDF within 1 second
- Customization settings must be applied to PDF generation within 1 second of an invoice being triggered
- Preview generation must complete in under 3 seconds
- Logo storage must survive account billing address changes without data loss

## Constraints

- PDF generation must use the existing PDF service — no new rendering infrastructure
- Logo storage must use the existing S3 infrastructure — no new object storage
- Customization applies to all invoices for the account; per-subscription overrides are out of scope

## Risks

- **Logo rendering degrades PDF quality** if customers upload oversized or poorly formatted images. Mitigation: server-side validation and resizing to a standard max width before storage.
- **PO number errors cause downstream AP problems** if customers mistype their PO number. Mitigation: the preview shows the PO number prominently; add a "confirm PO number" double-entry field.

## Milestones

### M1: Logo and PO Number (Week 1-4)

#### Deliverables

- Logo upload and storage via S3
- PO number field on account settings
- Invoice PDF rendering with logo and PO number
- Preview endpoint and preview UI

#### Acceptance Criteria

- Uploaded logo appears in invoice PDF preview within 3 seconds
- PO number appears in the "PO Number" field on generated PDF invoices
- Preview is pixel-accurate to the actual generated invoice

### M2: Address Override, Footer, and Regeneration (Week 5-7)

#### Deliverables

- Custom billing address override field
- Custom footer text field (500 chars)
- Regenerate most recent invoice with new settings action
- Customization change history log

#### Acceptance Criteria

- Custom billing address appears in the "Bill To" section of the invoice
- Footer text appears at the bottom of every generated invoice for the account
- Regenerate action produces a new PDF within 10 seconds and makes it available for download
